About VELORA

Built on trust. Driven by possibility.

VELORA Innovations Private Limited was formed to build and operate businesses that serve everyday needs and the operations behind them — from the produce on a family's table to the distribution, technology and infrastructure that make such supply possible.

We grow by entering sectors where we can work to a standard we are willing to put our name to, and by staying there for the long term.
