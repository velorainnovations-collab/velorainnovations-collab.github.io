Careers

Work where the details matter.

As our divisions grow, we look for people who take ownership of their work.

Working at VELORA

What we look for

Our businesses are operational. Stock has to be accurate, dispatches have to go out, sites have to be supervised, customers have to be called back. We value people who take responsibility, pay attention to details and complete their work with commitment.

Our team members are encouraged to take ownership, learn across different businesses and contribute to the company's growth. In return, you get real responsibility early and a direct line to the people making decisions.

Areas we hire for

Where you could fit.

We hire as our divisions require. These are the areas where openings usually arise.

Operations & warehousing

Stock handling, dispatch, documentation and day-to-day operations.

Sales & distribution

Retail and wholesale coverage, customer relationships and order management.

Procurement & sourcing

Supplier relationships, grading and quality checks for produce and essentials.

Technology

Web and software development, support and internal systems.

Projects & site work

Construction coordination, site supervision and project administration.

Accounts & administration

Billing, records, compliance support and office administration.
