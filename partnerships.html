Partnerships

Growing together.

We are open to partners who want a working relationship built on clarity and follow-through.

Why partner with us

One point of contact. Several capabilities.

Because VELORA operates across produce, distribution, technology, property, construction and transport, a partner often needs only one relationship where they would otherwise need several.

A brand that needs warehousing can also get secondary distribution and transport. A business that needs construction can also get the systems to run what gets built. We keep the coordination on our side.

Who we work with

Partnership opportunities.

Brands and manufacturers

Distribution, C&F services, warehousing and secondary distribution through Maa Enterprise, with transport support where we operate.

Suppliers and producers

Long-term sourcing relationships for fresh fruits, vegetables and daily essentials through VELORA Fresh.

Retailers and bulk buyers

Reliable supply of produce and essentials, with consistent grading and clear order communication.

Property and land partners

Collaboration on property opportunities and construction projects, assessed on documentation and long-term value.

Businesses needing technology

Websites, internal software, dashboards and support through our IT Solutions division.

Service collaborators

Contractors, transporters and specialists who hold the same standard of work that we expect of ourselves.

How to start

A short conversation first.

Tell us what you do and what you are looking for. If there is a genuine fit, we will say so quickly — and if there is not, we will say that too.
