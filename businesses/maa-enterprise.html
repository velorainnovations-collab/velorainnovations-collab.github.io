Overview

Maa Enterprise

Maa Enterprise is our distribution and supply-chain business. It operates as a distinct business under VELORA Innovations, serving brands and companies that need their products stored, handled and moved reliably.

Where VELORA Fresh is built around produce, Maa Enterprise is built around operations: stock accuracy, documentation, dispatch discipline and dependable coverage.

Services offered

  • FMCG distribution
  • Clearing and forwarding (C&F) services
  • Warehousing and stock handling
  • Supply-chain and order fulfilment support
  • Secondary distribution to retail and wholesale channels

What partners can expect

  • Accurate stock records and clean documentation
  • Defined dispatch cycles and order tracking
  • Careful handling and storage practices
  • A single point of contact for the whole operation
