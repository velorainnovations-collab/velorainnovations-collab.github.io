Overview

VELORA Fresh

VELORA Fresh is our fresh produce and daily essentials business. We source fruits, vegetables and everyday household essentials, and move them to customers quickly enough to protect quality at every step.

Produce is unforgiving: handling, temperature and time decide what the customer finally receives. Our work is built around keeping that chain short and well managed.

What this division handles

  • Sourcing of fresh fruits and vegetables
  • Daily essentials and household staples
  • Grading, sorting and quality checks before dispatch
  • Supply to retail counters, bulk buyers and institutional customers

How we work

  • Short, well-managed handling cycles to protect freshness
  • Consistent grading so customers know what to expect
  • Transparent pricing and clear order communication
  • Flexible order sizes for both regular and one-off requirements
