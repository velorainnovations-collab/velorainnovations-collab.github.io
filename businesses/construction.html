Overview

Construction

Our Construction division handles construction and infrastructure-related work. The priorities here are simple and they do not change: a realistic plan, correct materials, safe sites and work completed to the standard that was agreed.

Scope and timelines are set honestly at the start, so that progress can be measured against them.

Work undertaken

  • Construction and civil works
  • Infrastructure-related projects
  • Project planning and site coordination
  • Material sourcing and contractor management

How projects are managed

  • Clear scope, costing and schedule before work begins
  • Defined site supervision and quality checks
  • Safety practices maintained on site
  • Regular progress updates to the client
